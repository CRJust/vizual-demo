# Bvyvizual

BEAVY — PRODUCT PRESENTATION WEBSITE
Build a premium, minimalist product presentation website for Beavy, a Romanian grocery price comparison platform.
Brand: Beavy
Domain: beavy.ro
Contact: contact@beavy.ro
Tagline: Cumpărături chibzuite. Fără risipă.
Main objective
Present the actual Beavy product, explain its value and demonstrate how users can compare grocery prices across stores.
This is a product presentation website, NOT a pre-launch landing page.
Website sections
Hero — clear product positioning and direct access to the application.
Product showcase — real screenshots of Beavy's interface.
How it works — add products, compare prices, choose the best offers.
Core capabilities — product matching, basket comparison, store selection and online/physical price distinction.
Footer — contact and essential company information.
Do not include FAQ, beta registration, newsletter, lead-generation forms, testimonials, blog or invented statistics.
Visual direction
Create a premium European SaaS aesthetic.
Use warm white, cream, dark forest green and subtle neutral tones.
The Beavy beaver mascot should be a secondary brand element. Preserve its recognizable identity from the supplied references, but refine it with natural anatomy, realistic fur, balanced proportions and restrained facial expression.
Avoid childish cartoon styling, oversized eyes, exaggerated expressions, Pixar-like rendering and excessive mascot use.
No decorative animations, parallax, bouncing elements or distracting transitions.
Product accuracy
Use actual Beavy screenshots and verified functionality.
Never invent prices, retailer integrations, savings or product capabilities.
Use clearly labelled placeholders only when real data is unavailable.
Technical
React + TypeScript, responsive, accessible, SEO-ready and deployable on Cloudflare.
Keep the existing Beavy application, database, DNS and Workers untouched. Build the presentation website separately.
Success criteria: A visitor understands what Beavy does, sees how the product works and can access the actual application.       Please remove the entire Project Status (Beta) concept from the website.
Do not mention TestFlight, Google Play Beta, or any beta testing channels.
Do not include email or city registration forms, waitlists, or lead-generation features.
Do not position the website as a pre-launch or beta announcement landing page.
Beavy should be presented as an actual product. The website's sole purpose is to showcase the platform, explain its features, demonstrate how it works, and direct visitors to the application.
Keep the presentation focused on the product itself, not its launch status or user acquisition.
